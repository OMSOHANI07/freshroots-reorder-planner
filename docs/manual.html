1. How it works

Seven steps. The colour shows who does each one.

AnalystPlanner (automatic)RaghavProcurement · ERPNext

2. Signing in

Open the website and press Sign in. Choose your role: Analyst, Raghav Kulkarni or Procurement. Your Home screen then shows your one next step.

The sign-in is a demo role picker for now. Add real logins before using it with live suppliers.

3. Analyst: run the plan

  1. Go to New run.
  2. Choose the data: Latest SAP data (files already uploaded; shows when and by whom), or Upload new files and drop the five CSVs exported from SAP: weekly_sales.csv, sku_master.csv, current_inventory.csv, calendar.csv, distributor_master.csv.
  3. Press Run the plan. The 12 data checks tick through one by one.
  4. All passed: the plan opens. You're done; it appears on Raghav's Home screen.
  5. A check failed: no plan is made. Read the red message, fix the file, run again.

"Stock data is recent" failed? The stock date in current_inventory.csv is more than 7 days before today. Upload fresh data, or open More options and set the Run date within a week of the stock date (for replaying old data).

Optional · More options → Send a SKU to Raghav: add a SKU, a reason the data can't show (for example, a competitor promotion) and a valid-until date. That SKU isn't ordered automatically.

4. Raghav: approve or reject

  1. Home shows "Plan #N needs your decision". Press Review the plan.
  2. Read the three headline numbers: SKUs to reorder, units to order, SKUs flagged.
  3. Read the flagged SKUs in the decision panel. Tap any SKU for 16 weeks of sales, stock cover before and after the order, the rules that fired, and the plain-language reason.
  4. Approve: press Approve plan, add an optional note, press Confirm approval. Purchase orders are raised straight away.
  5. Reject: press Reject plan, say why (required), press Confirm rejection. Nothing is ordered.

A single SKU's quantity can't be changed. To change something, reject the plan with a note and ask the Analyst to re-run.

5. Procurement: purchase orders

  1. Home opens the latest approved plan; or go to Plans and pick one marked Approved.
  2. Each supplier has one purchase-order tile with its lines, total units and status.
  3. In ERPNext as PUR-ORD-… — created in ERPNext as a Draft. Press Open in ERPNext, add rates and the real Required By date, then submit it.
  4. ERPNext sync failed — the tile says why (for example, a supplier or item missing in ERPNext). Fix it in ERPNext, then press Retry ERPNext.
  5. ERPNext not connected — use the downloads, enter the PO in ERPNext by hand, then press Entered manually and type the reference.
  6. Download gives a PDF, Excel or CSV of one PO; All as ZIP gives everything for the plan.

There are no prices or lead times in the source data. Add them in ERPNext before submitting a PO.

6. Data

The Data page shows what the latest plan was built on. It's read-only and changes only with the next SAP upload.

  • SKUs — name, supplier, perishability, standard order quantity, stock on hand, 16-week sales line.
  • Suppliers — name, region, and how many SKUs each supplies.
  • Festival calendar — festival months (expected demand +25%).
  • Overrides — SKUs sent to Raghav by hand.

7. What the colours mean

StatusMeaningWhat happens
Stockout riskStock lasts less than the minimum weeksReorder now: standard quantity, or 1.5× if sales are up more than 20%
OverstockStock lasts more than the maximum weeksPause the next order
HealthyStock cover is inside the windowNo order now; next order 1.5× if sales are rising
Judgment zoneThe rules can't decideNothing ordered automatically

Healthy windows: long-life SKUs 6–14 weeks of stock; perishable SKUs 3–4 weeks.

8. What the flags mean

A flag never changes a quantity; it asks for a closer look.

FlagMeaning
R1Even after the order, stock stays below the minimum
R2Within half a week of a limit: a close call
R3Sales spiked in a festival month; the uplift may count twice
R4The order pushes stock above the maximum
J1New product, or less than 12 weeks of sales
J2Sent to Raghav by hand, with a reason
J3No sales in 12 weeks, so cover can't be worked out

9. Questions

Who decides how much to order?
The rules engine, using Raghav's thresholds. Raghav approves or rejects the plan. AI only explains.
Who can change a rule or threshold?
Only Raghav, with sign-off from Deepa Nair (Head of Operations). See RULEBOOK.md, Section 16. Procurement and the Analyst can't.
When is a month a festival month?
Only when the festival calendar marks it. Today: March, October and November. Expected demand goes up 25%.
Why are there no prices on the POs?
The SAP files have no unit costs, and the planner never guesses. Add rates in ERPNext.
What if ERPNext is down or not connected?
The approval still stands and the POs are kept. Each one shows the reason and can be retried or entered by hand.
Where is the history?
History shows every plan, the rejection rate (the kill line is 40%), and the audit log of who did what and when.
